My Journey

Way back in the golden days of MySpace, when profile tweaks were all the rage, a spark was ignited. That spark? A boundless curiosity about the inner workings of web apps. Fast forward to today, and that curiosity has blossomed into a full-blown passion for web development.

These days, I’m on a mission to craft delightfully user-friendly interfaces using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. My ultimate quest is to become a master of both front-end and back-end development, conjuring up tools and experiences that genuinely make life a tad easier for everyone.

Right now, I'm diving into the realms of React, Bootstrap, and backend magic with Node.jsand Express.js.All the while, I'm making sure my designs stay as clean as a whistle and as accessible as a well-loved public library.
